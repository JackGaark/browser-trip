raster: tiles in parallel

Pixels, one tile at a time

Each layer is cut into tiles, and several raster workers fill them in parallel, nearest the viewport first. Scroll faster than they can keep up and you see blank checkerboard tiles for a moment.

Then the compositor, running on the GPU, stacks the finished tiles into a frame. On a 60 Hz screen it gets 16.7 ms to do that, every single frame.
